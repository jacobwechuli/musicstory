HI, I'M TED.

I love music. Very much.

Music has gotten me through a lot of dark times. For me, it isn't simply something I listen to for entertainment — it runs my life. I mark moments with music. I go for walks built entirely around a set of songs, because they're better with music. I go to certain places for the music. I even drink because certain songs sound better while drunk.

It's almost like everyone in my life has a theme song. When I hear it, I think of them — and when I stop liking them, I stop liking the song.

My career, I code because of the music. My greatest goal in life has always been to work at Spotify — or build my own. While people marvel at how revolutionary AI is, my Mount Rushmore in tech has always been Poweramp. It's what steered me into digital signal processing, and everything that comes with it.

“If I send you a song, that's the greatest compliment you could get from me.”

This is my story. I'm Ted, and I love music.
